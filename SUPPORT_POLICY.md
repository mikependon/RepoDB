# Our Support Policy

RepoDB is primarily maintained by a single entity and this entity is a person and not an organization. Though, the .NET open-source community can help support the library in an open-source context.

When providing operational support, we will not enforce any monetary contributions from the other party, as all kinds of support are always free.

## Support Process

### Kinds of Support

- Inquiry - operational support
- Development
- Implementation
- Pull-Requests and Reviews
- Enhancements
- Debugging/Tracing
- Bug Fixing
- Deployment

### Daily Support

- All kinds of operational support must be within the Central European Time (CET) timezone.
- The inquiry support can be anytime except from 10pm to 5am of the next day (CET) daily.
- The other kinds of support will be done in between 5am to 7am and/or 10pm to 12am (CET) daily.

### Weekend Support

- All kinds of operational support must be within the Central European Time (CET) timezone.
- The inquiry support can be anytime except from 10pm to 5am of the next day (CET) daily.
- The other kinds of support will be done in between 5am to 7am, 1pm to 3pm and/or 10pm to 12am (CET) daily.

### Disclaimer

The maintainer has the right to say NO to any kind of support during the work hours (weekdays) from 7am to 4pm (CET) of the mentioned timezone. However, the open-source community has the right to support the library (anytime, anywhere).

## Consultancy

In the event of consultancy, as needed by the other party, a schedule can be defined in advance by collaborating directly with the author.

## Channels

We strongly recommend that the .NET community always reach out through our proper [channels](https://github.com/mikependon/RepoDB#engagements). Alternatively, reach us directly via [X / Twitter](https://twitter.com/mike_pendon) or [email](mailto:michael.c.pendon@outlook.com).

## Governance

Until the proper governance and entitlement of the library in the legal/trademark (conflict of interests, taxation, etc.) are defined, the support will remain as it is.
